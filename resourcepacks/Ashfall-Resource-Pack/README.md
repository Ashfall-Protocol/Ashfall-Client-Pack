# ASHFALL PROTOCOL // OFFICIAL RESOURCE PACK (v1.0)
Tactical Military Overhaul for Minecraft Forge 1.20.1.

## Modules Included:
- **GUI & Containers:** Dark military interfaces for containers, inventory, dispenser, hopper, widgets, and status effects. (Inspired by Tarkovchanin GUI by KDjFCzoW).
- **Armor & Tactical Rig:** Military plate carriers, combat helmets, tactical vests, and camouflage field dress. (Inspired by AORKOS' Military Armor-set).
- **World & Industrial Blocks:** Worn, scratched heavy metal, iron blocks, blast furnaces, and bunker blast doors. (Inspired by Eyeful Iron).
- **TaCZ Tactical Sprint:** Modern tactical sprint animations for firearms. (By Zendy / TAC SPRINT TaCZ Edition).
- **Custom Items & Lore (CustomModelData):**
  - Tripwire Hook (CMD 1001): Airdrop Electronic Breach Key
  - Paper (CMD 2001-2004): Military Keycards (Red Omega, Blue Comms, Green Armory, Black Director)
  - Iron Nugget (CMD 3001): Ashfall Salvage Scrip / Buyer Token
  - Gold Nugget (CMD 4001): Tactical Clan Core Transponder
  - Nether Star (CMD 5001-5005): Donor Rank Badges (Contractor, Field Agent, Specialist, Commander, Director)
- **Branding & Tactical Font:** Custom military glyphs and official Ashfall emblem.
